New Directions

Concept A

The Diamond

Elevates the ΠΚΑ diamond emblem to a full tournament seal. The four shield petals frame a maroon center field — same logic as the fraternity sticker but with circular ring text and gold ring. Highest PIKE DNA, most fraternity-forward.

Concept B

The Major

A proper major championship seal. Maroon ring with gold arc text, parchment interior with crossed clubs and a flag. Augusta DNA — the outer-ring-inner-field format is how the Masters, U.S. Open, and The Open all do it. Cleanest, most "official tournament" feel.

Concept C

The Crest

Full PIKE heraldic treatment — knight helmet, sunburst, ΠΚΑ dagger shield, lily of the valley sprigs adapted with crossed golf clubs, ribbon banner. Closest to the actual PIKE emblem. Most ornate, best as a printed item or embroidered patch.

Concept D

The Wordmark

Channel the PIKE SLAG identity — all type, no illustration. Big maroon "1813" does all the work; "CLASSIC" sits in spaced Oswald caps below. Clean, confident, prints cleanly on any surface. Most versatile: scorecards, koozies, shirts, hats.

Notes

All concepts use the same maroon/gold/parchment palette as the current site. Concepts A and C lean into PIKE fraternity heraldry. B and D are tournament-forward and produce cleaner merch. The current site badge is closest to C — any of these could replace or supplement it.
